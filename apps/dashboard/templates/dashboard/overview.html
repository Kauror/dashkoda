{# ---------------- Auditooriumid ---------------- #} {% comment %} The heading keeps the id `section-channels`. The section has been `Kanalite statistika`, `Kanalite auditoorium` and `Kanalid`; the anchor is a contract other tests scope themselves to, because this is the one place on the page where a hand-typed figure must never borrow a synchronised feed's vocabulary and that check needs to address exactly this section. Three things this strip must keep doing: - **never total the newsletters.** Three lists whose overlap nobody has measured, so the newsletter cell lists each on its own and carries no headline figure at all. A sum would silently claim the overlap is zero; - **never imply the social figures are synchronised.** They are typed by a staff user. The words `sünkroonitud`, `API-ga ühendatud` and `automaatselt uuendatud` appear nowhere near them, and a channel nobody has entered reads `Andmed puuduvad`, never `0`; - **never repeat the website.** Sessions are the `Koduleht ja uudised` card's headline; `build_channel_band(include_website=False)` leaves the slot out at the source rather than the template hiding it, so the query does not run either. It is deliberately the quietest section on the page and deliberately last: audience sizes are context, and the operational intelligence above them is what the page is for. Compact rows rather than the six large cards this band used to be — those took more homepage space than any other section and said less than any of them. {% endcomment %}
