{# -------- Liikmeskond -------- #} {% comment %} The board report's own figures: its member total, its paid count and the fee collection those two are read alongside. Every figure here comes off one monthly report and says so beneath itself. The koda.ee directory total is not repeated here. It is a different definition on a different cadence, it leads the headline strip, and stating it twice on one page is what invites a reader to subtract one total from the other. The paid share is computed inside the board report alone — dividing the internal paid count by the public directory total would be a ratio between two different definitions and is never shown. Both trends run on one pair of axes, as a solid and a dashed line. The board reads this card to see the gap between the two, and two separate drawings with two independent scales made that gap a matter of guesswork. Sharing the axes is a drawing decision and nothing more: the lines keep their own labels, their own sources and their own cadences, they are never summed, and neither is ever continued into the other. {% endcomment %}
