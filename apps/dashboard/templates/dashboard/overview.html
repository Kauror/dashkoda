{% extends "dashboard/shell.html" %} {% comment %} Ülevaade — the board's landing page. Layout follows direction 1a of the Overview Refresh design: a headline strip, one activity window, a two-by-two grid of module cards, and the communication channels that have no source yet. Everything it renders comes from `apps.dashboard.overview.build_overview`, which reads PostgreSQL through each module's own selectors. The template chooses layout and nothing else. Three things are deliberate and should stay that way: - a figure with no connected source renders its empty state, never a zero; - the member total and the paid-member figure are drawn as two sources with their own names and their own update cadence. They count different things and are never added, compared or continued into one another; - the activity strip uses one fixed window, so all of its counts describe the same period. The member delta, whose baseline is the previous reading rather than a period, is shown beside the member figure with its own date. The mockup this follows carries synthetic numbers and says so on its own banner. None of them reached this file. {% endcomment %} {% block title %}Ülevaade – DashKoda{% endblock %} {% block content %}
