{% comment %} One domain card of `Põhinäitajad`. Context: card – an `ExecutiveDomainCard` Six of these sit in a grid — three columns on a desktop, two on a tablet, one on a phone — so the card is compact by contract rather than by taste. Its shape is fixed and identical on all six, which is what lets a reader scan them: the domain, the figure, the comparison, the supporting facts, and a footer holding the period on the left and the way out on the right. What this card deliberately does not render: - a **question line** or a **meaning sentence**. Both restated the figure immediately above them. The board struck the questions on 2026-08-15 and the sentences went with the tall pillar card on 2026-08-17; - a **sparkline**. Six cards fit two rows only because none of them is tall, and a trend a couple of centimetres wide cannot be read anyway; - a **source caption under every number**. Provenance lives in the data — `ExecutiveMetric` and `ExecutiveFact` still carry `source` and `as_of`, and `Andmete seis` at `/haldus/` is built from the same objects. What the card shows instead is one `period_line`, which the builder composes so that a card mixing two sources (Liikmeskond) can name both cadences in one short line; - a **status colour for the card as a whole**. There is no red/amber/green verdict on a domain. What needs attention is in `Tähelepanu` above, decided by the domain that measured it. Direction is never colour alone: the comparison text carries its own sign. {% endcomment %}
