Context: figure – a SourcedFigure from `apps.dashboard.overview` Why not `chart_figure`: ECharts is a large bundle and the overview is the page every viewer loads first. A card-sized line of a dozen points does not justify shipping a charting library, so the coordinates are computed in `apps/dashboard/sparkline.py` and emitted as a plain ``. Geometry travels in the `points` attribute, never in a `style`. That is what keeps `style-src 'self'` intact. The same contract as `chart_figure` applies to the alternative: the summary and the data table are **not** a fallback for a broken canvas — they stay in the document for every reader. Only the drawing is optional, and when there is not enough data to describe a trend nothing is drawn at all rather than an axis with a single dot on it. {% endcomment %}
