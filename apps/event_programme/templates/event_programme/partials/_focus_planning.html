It is not when a public page appeared, and nothing here calls it a publication date: those are different measurements and only the first has a source. `planning_lead_days` is the generator's own `start_date − added_date`. It is reproduced rather than recomputed, and the identity was verified against the real export on every row carrying both dates. Events entered *after* they began carry a negative lead. They are shown as the data-quality fact they are and excluded from the statistics — a negative planning lead is not a short one, it is a different thing — and nothing clamps them to zero. Prices are the programme's **planned** prices. They are not transactions, not revenue, and not what anybody was charged. A price nobody recorded is never read as free.
