A change carries a **glyph as well as a colour**, because a change distinguished only by hue does not exist for a reader who cannot separate the hues, and `change_label` is what a screen reader gets instead of an arrow it cannot describe. Direction is not judgement: a fall in traffic is not an error and a rise is not a success, so the glyph says which way and nothing calls it good. A headline with **no** change is a headline whose comparison was refused. The reason is printed in its place rather than left as an absence.
