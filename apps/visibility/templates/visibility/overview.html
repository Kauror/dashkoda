{% extends "dashboard/shell.html" %} {% comment %} Nähtavus — how many people the Chamber currently reaches. Everything here comes from `apps.visibility.page.build_visibility_page`, which reads PostgreSQL through the app's own selectors. The template lays out and holds no rule. Four things are deliberate and should stay that way: - **every figure on this page was typed by a person.** Each one carries "Käsitsi sisestatud" and the date it describes. Nothing says synchronised, API-connected or automatically updated, because none of it is; - the three newsletters are listed one by one and **never totalled**. They go to three separate lists and nobody has counted how many people are on more than one, so a sum would silently claim that overlap is zero; - a channel nobody has entered says "Andmed puuduvad". Never a zero; - trends are server-drawn sparklines and each one keeps its values as a table. The table is not a fallback: it stays in the document for every reader. {% endcomment %} {% block title %}Nähtavus – DashKoda{% endblock %} {% block content %}
