{% comment %} `Tellitud väärtus` — what value has been ordered through the shop, and what it is made of. Every figure on this view is **ordered value excluding VAT**: the sum of unit price × quantity over completed Commerce orders. It is not revenue, not turnover, and not money received. Koda.ee records no payment receipt and has no refund or cancellation concept at all, and roughly 77% of orders are invoices the website marks as sent and never as paid. Whether an invoice was settled is the accounting system's fact and this application does not have it. Volume and value are deliberately shown as two different questions. The products people take most often and the products carrying the most ordered value are not the same list, and a single ranking would quietly answer only one. {% endcomment %}
