Context: kpis – a tuple of page.KpiCard (three, since 2026-08-18) comparison_available – whether a previous equal-length window exists at all mix – page.MixPresenter, the fourth card Direction is an arrow **and** a sign as well as a colour, so the change survives greyscale, a monochrome print and colour blindness. The three states below are deliberately distinct, and none of them is a zero: * a change, where a previous window exists and both figures are real; * `Muutust ei saa arvutada`, where the window exists but the arithmetic has no answer — a previous value of zero has no percentage; * `Võrdlusperiood ei mahu ajalukku`, where a full equal-length previous window would reach behind the imported history. Comparing a year against the 83 days that happen to precede it is worse than showing nothing, because the reader cannot see that the denominator was short. `dk-kpi-strip` alone divides four cards evenly at `xl` — three KPIs and the mix card, with no modifier needed the way three cards alone once needed `dk-kpi-strip-trio` to avoid an empty fourth cell. {% endcomment %}
