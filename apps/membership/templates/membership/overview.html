Avalik liikmekataloog — how many member profiles Koda.ee publishes. Its definition text is unchanged from before the internal history existed. 2. Sisemine liikmeskonna aruanne — what the Chamber's own board reports say. They count different things. Nothing here adds them, subtracts them, or presents one as a continuation of the other, and when the two numbers differ the page says why the definitions differ rather than implying an error. There is still deliberately no "uusi liikmeid sel aastal" figure from the public source — it is not a DashKoda metric. The internal new-member figures below come from the board reports and are labelled as internal reported data.
