Two focuses since 2026-08-18, not five. `Ülevaade` answers what needs attention right now — the headline, the standing lists, and the whole register, searchable and filterable, no longer one click away behind its own focus. `Töövoog ja arvamused` answers how the register moves over time — both halves of it, arrival and dispatch, in one scroll instead of two. `Arvamused`, `Liikmete tagasiside` and `Register` retired as separate focuses the same day; what each drew either moved onto one of the two survivors or came off the page entirely — see `intelligence_page.py` for exactly which. `fookus` chooses which of the two is drawn, so every view is bookmarkable, shareable and reload-safe, and the back button moves between them like it moves between any two pages. There is no SPA and no client-side routing: the server renders the focus it was asked for. Every value comes from one imported snapshot, and the page reads PostgreSQL and nothing else.
