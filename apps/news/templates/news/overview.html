Everything that used to sit between the page title and the first article — a source-status panel, two KPI cards repeating the feed's length, and a summary paragraph under every headline — is gone, and the list starts within a screen of the heading. Three presentational rules carry the density: * the unit is named once, in the column heading. `4 lehevaatamist` under every article is the same fact spelled a hundred times, and it cost a line each; * the GA4 coverage caveat is stated once beneath the list rather than under every article older than the measurement; * no summary, and no category *badge*. The summary is still stored and the article is one click away. The category is a filter above the list rather than a mark on every row: it takes two values, so a badge would repeat one of two words a thousand times down the page while answering a question the chips already answer in one click. External links keep the whole contract: `target="_blank"`, `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, a visually hidden note naming the destination and the new tab, and `relative` on the anchor so that note stays inside the scrolling wrapper. {% endcomment %} {% block title %}Uudised – DashKoda{% endblock %} {% block content %}
